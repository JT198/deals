How Deal Finder works

What it does

It watches Facebook Marketplace and Craigslist within your radius (Setup tab) for 4-seat and 2-seat UTVs, ATVs, 3-wheelers and zero-turn mowers. It reads every ad, works out what similar machines usually list for, and flags the ones priced well below that. Private sellers are the focus; dealers are shown but marked.

How often it looks

  • Full scan every 20 minutes, 6am-11pm: all searches on Craigslist; on Facebook, 4-seat UTV and mower searches every scan and the other categories hourly.
  • ⚡ Fast lane every 5 minutes: only the searches marked ⚡ in Setup, newest first, so a new listing usually shows up 5-10 minutes after it's posted.
  • For each new ad it opens the listing, then a local AI model reads it and pulls out the year, model, miles/hours, deck size, add-ons, whether it's a dealer, and any problems (red flags). Parts, "wanted" ads and the wrong kind of machine are dropped.

"Typical" price

  • The middle asking price of the same model family (e.g. RZR XP 4, Toro TimeCutter) seen in the last 6 months, within a model year either side, adjusted about 8% per year of age. Mowers are matched on deck size when there are enough. Vintage 3-wheelers don't lose value with age, so no age adjustment for those.
  • Adjusted for use: when the ad states miles or hours, similar machines are lined up to the same use before taking the middle price. How much a mile or an hour is worth is learned from the listings for that model, starting from a sensible default (about 2.5% per 1,000 miles for UTVs, 4% per 100 hours for mowers). The grey line under "Typical" shows the adjustment.
  • These are asking prices - sold prices aren't public - so "typical" means what these usually list for. With few listings of a model the number is rougher; the comps count tells you how many it's based on.
  • New/dealer-stock units get no typical price (used comps don't apply); compare those to MSRP.

Score (the number on each card)

  • 50 is a normally priced listing. 60-74 "Good", 75+ "Great".
  • Goes up for being under typical, price cuts, and signs the seller is motivated (OBO, must sell). Goes down for dealers, new stock, and being over typical.
  • Anything with red flags (needs work, title problems, scam signs) is capped at 70 - never "Great".
  • Prices under $100 or under 20% of typical are treated as placeholders ("$1 - make an offer"), not deals.
  • Few comps or no model year pull the score toward the middle, because the typical price is less certain.

Offer guidance

  • Walk away above - the typical price for this machine (or the asking price, if lower). Paying more means you're paying above market.
  • Aim to pay - normally 7% under that, more when the seller is motivated, it's a dealer, or it has sat for 3+ weeks; less when it was just listed (other buyers are coming).
  • Open with - a little under your aim, never below 75% of asking.
  • If it's already 15%+ under typical, the guidance flips: offer close to asking and move fast - lowballing a real deal usually loses it.
  • Red flags: get a repair estimate and take it off these numbers. Always check the title/VIN in person.

Telegram alerts

  • Deal alerts - private listings scoring at or above the threshold in Setup (75). Sent again if the score jumps 10+ points (e.g. a price cut).
  • 🆕 Just-listed alerts - fresh private listings (posted within 2 hours) that are priced normally or better and have no red flags, so you can be first to message. On for 4-seat UTVs and mowers; change per category in Setup.
  • Per category you can turn each alert type on or off and set a max price and minimum year. Listings far outside the radius and cross-posts of something already sent are skipped.

Using the dashboard

  • Category bar picks what you're looking at; model chips narrow it to specific families.
  • "Listed in the last hour" / "new since your last visit" in the header are quick views - click to see just those, click again (or ✕) to go back. Your filters aren't changed.
  • NEW marks listings added since your last visit in this browser; Just listed = posted within a day.
  • Star to keep track of one, Hide to get it out of the way (Show hidden brings them back). Stars and hides are shared by everyone who uses this dashboard.
  • Market tab: every asking price seen for a model by year - handy for knowing what yours is worth, too.
  • Setup: radius and home ZIP, alert rules, searches (add your own; ⚡ puts one in the fast lane), and recent scan history with any problems.

Limits worth knowing

  • Facebook is read without an account. If Facebook starts blocking it, Setup → Recent scans will say so.
  • OfferUp and ATVTrader aren't covered (they block this kind of reading).
  • The AI occasionally misreads an ad (wrong model family, 2-seat vs 4-seat). The card summary and the listing itself are the final word.
